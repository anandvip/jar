💡 What Is This Tool?

🎮 Think of it as a Financial Time Machine

This tool helps you see if your money will last through your entire retirement. It's like a calculator that acts as a time machine — you put your financial life in, and it shows you the next 30-50 years.

⭐ The Simple Story (Explained Like You're in 5th Grade)

Imagine you want to run your home for many years using your savings. You don't want your money to finish early.

To manage this, you:

  1. Decide your monthly spending (₹1,00,000)
  2. Add your savings (₹3,00,00,000)
  3. See how much money grows every year (returns)
  4. Divide your money into 6 jars (NEC, PLAY, FFA, etc.)
  5. Spend from some jars, and save and grow the others
  6. Check if the money survives 30–50 years
  7. Add special events (daughter's wedding, son's setup)
  8. See charts and results

✨ This whole tool is doing just these 8 things — nothing more magical than that!

🧠 The 4 Most Important Functions

You don't need to understand the code — just understand what these 4 functions do:

Function 1 applyTaxation()

Think: This is like a teacher checking your marks

• MF (Mutual Funds) earns 12% → teacher cuts 10% tax → you get 10.8%
• FD (Fixed Deposit) earns 7% → teacher cuts 25% tax → you get 5.25%

Purpose: Makes the returns realistic after taxation (LTCG and TDS)

Function 2 getJarAllocations()

Think: This divides your income into 6 jars

If you earn ₹1,00,000/month: • NEC = ₹55,000 • FFA = ₹10,000 • LTSS = ₹10,000 • EDU = ₹10,000 • PLAY = ₹10,000 • GIVE = ₹5,000

Purpose: Shows how much money each jar gets every month

Function 3 runUnifiedLongevitySimulation()

Think: This is the heart of the tool — imagine your savings is a water tank

Each year:
• 💧 Water comes IN (investment returns)
• 💧 Water goes OUT (your expenses)
• 💧 Some water is poured into jars (savings jars)
• 💧 Jars also grow like smaller water bottles

The Check: Will the big tank become empty early? Or will it survive all years?
If empty → ❌ FAIL | If water lasts → ✅ PASS

Function 4 runBaselineProjection() + runRealisticMonteCarlo()

Think: These show the graph of your future

• Baseline = calm weather (stable returns)
• Monte Carlo = storms, rains, droughts (good/bad years randomly)

Purpose: Draw a line on the chart showing your money level every year for 5–50 years

⚙️ How It Actually Works (Step by Step)

1

You Enter Your Numbers

Monthly need: ₹1,00,000 | Capital: ₹2 Crores | Age: 45 | MF/FD split: 60/40

2

Tool Calculates Your Returns

Each month, ₹1,00,000 split across 6 jars. Some jars accumulate (FFA, LTSS, EDU), others are spent (NEC, GIVE, PLAY)

6

Results Displayed

Chart shows corpus trajectory | Jar balances visible | CSV export available | Pass/Fail verdict

📊 Understanding the Outputs

✅ What "Pass" Means

When the tool says "✓ Survives 30 years", it means:
Your corpus (savings) generates enough returns every year to cover your expenses for all 30 years, even after accounting for inflation, healthcare costs, and special goals like weddings.

⚠️ What "Fail" Means

When the tool says "✖ Capital depleted at Year 18", it means:
Your corpus runs out of money in Year 18 because withdrawals + expenses exceed returns. You need to either reduce spending, increase capital, or adjust your expectations.

📈 Reading the Chart

The line going down = corpus reducing over time
The line going up = corpus growing
Flat line = balanced (withdrawals equal returns)

Colored lines: Show individual jar balances (FFA, LTSS, EDU)

🎲 Monte Carlo Results

Instead of one future, Monte Carlo shows 100 possible futures with good and bad years mixed.

Median (50th %ile): Middle outcome
Best (90th %ile): Optimistic scenario
Worst (10th %ile): Pessimistic scenario
Failure Rate: How many of the 100 runs depleted capital
